Chintu AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Chintu AI runs AI models on your own device. We built it so that your conversations stay with you.

What stays on your device

Your messages, the AI's replies, photos and PDFs you attach, your settings and your chat history are processed and stored only on your device, inside the app's storage. We do not have servers that receive them, and we cannot see them.

What goes over the internet

  • AI model downloads. When you download a model, the app fetches it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and the AI engine from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). These services see a normal download request, such as your IP address. No messages are sent.
  • Update checks. The app asks jsDelivr which AI engine version is newest.
  • Web lookup (off by default). If you turn it on, the words of your question are sent to Wikipedia (wikipedia.org) to search for background facts.
  • Voice typing. If you use the microphone button, your browser's speech service converts speech to text. On Chrome this is handled by Google.
  • Reports. If you tap Report and then choose to email it, the reply you flagged is sent by your own email app to us.

What we collect

Nothing. Chintu AI has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear chat history, or delete models from the Models screen. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data removes everything.

Children

Chintu AI is not directed at children under 13.
